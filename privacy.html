Privacy & enquiries

Updated 25 October 2026

Contacting Axyntra

The enquiry options prepare a message in WhatsApp or your email application. Review the message before sending it. Information you send may include your name, email address and project requirements and is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, email providers and external social links operate under their own privacy terms. Some pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Cloudflare-hosted libraries; these requests can disclose technical information such as your IP address to those providers. The hosting provider may also process technical request logs.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive access credentials through the initial enquiry. Discuss an appropriate access-sharing method if the project needs it.

Questions and requests

Contact info@axyntra.lk with questions about information you have shared, including requests to correct or delete it. Retention for an active project or business records should be agreed with Axyntra.
