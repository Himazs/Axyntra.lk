Our story

Small beginnings.
Forward thinking.

Axyntra Digital Solutions was founded by Mohommad Himas in November 2023, with a focus on helping businesses build a useful and professional online presence.

Based in Sri Lanka and open to projects worldwide, we work on custom business websites, e-commerce experiences and practical web applications. Each project begins with understanding what the business needs and what its customers expect.

Our approach combines thoughtful design with hands-on development. We define the work clearly, collaborate through the build and review the agreed features before launch.
